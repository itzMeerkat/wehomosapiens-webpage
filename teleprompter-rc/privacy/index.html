Privacy Policy

Teleprompter with RC

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary

Teleprompter with RC does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your own devices.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information or usage data, and the app contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.

How the App Works With Your Content

  • Scripts and settings. The text you write or import, and your preferences, are stored locally on your device. We have no access to them.
  • Apple Watch remote control. When you control the teleprompter from an Apple Watch, communication happens directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's system frameworks. No data is sent to us or to any server.
  • Floating window. The teleprompter text is displayed on your screen on top of other apps, such as camera apps. The app does not read, record, or capture the content of other apps.

Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The app does not include third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at admin@wehomosapiens.com.
